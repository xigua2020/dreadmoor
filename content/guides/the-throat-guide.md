The Throat is the gate out of the demo region — and the puzzle that stalls most captains. Here is the full sequence, with the checks that matter when the gate refuses to move. Prerequisites: [Hull Protection and the Upgraded Engine](/guides/boat-upgrades-guide/) installed.

## Step 1: Fuel and Start the Generator

Dock at The Throat and find the **Generator**. It needs fuel, but you do not sail back for it — search the nearby working area for a **Fuel Can**, refuel and start the generator. Try the **Gate Control**: the gate fails, and the objective changes to repairing the waterway machinery.

## Step 2: The Four Machinery Repairs

- **Thermal Coil** — install it in the damaged electrical assembly.
- **Gear** — install the replacement gear in the designated gear slot.
- **Buckets ×2** — install one in each water-wheel mounting point. One bucket is not enough, and this is the most commonly missed part.
- **Water Pump** — repair it **in place**. Do not craft a replacement at John's Lathe; the objective tracks the on-site repair.

## Step 3: Route Water to Both Wheels

Use the four **water-flow controls** and the glowing **blue pivots** to route water through the channels. The **Hook Gun** pulls the pivots that move the planks. The goal is flow to **both** wheels — wait until both are visibly turning.

## Step 4: Open the Gate

Return to the **Gate Control** and open the passage. Board the boat, follow the opened route and sail until the Thank You for Playing screen appears — that is the end of the October 2026 demo.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| Gate Control does nothing | Generator not fueled or not started |
| "Repair machinery" objective stuck | Missing Thermal Coil, Gear, a Bucket, or the in-place pump repair |
| One wheel turning | Second Bucket missing, or flow not routed to the second wheel |
| "Configure water flow" stuck | Channel controls and pivots — not a generator problem |
| Pump repaired but objective unchanged | A replacement was crafted instead of repairing the pump in place |
