DREADMOOR's interface is diegetic — most controls appear as prompts on the object you are looking at. This reference collects the controls that matter, grouped by what you are doing.

## At the Helm

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| Nitro | Short speed burst — best saved for escapes and open water |
| Spotlight | Toggles the boat lamp for night water |
| Dock | Moors the boat at a dock prompt |
| F — Leave Helm | Steps away from the wheel so you can move aboard |

The compass sits at the top of the screen, and the quest marker shows direction and distance. Watch both before open-water runs.

## On Foot and Aboard

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| F | Universal interact: install modules, open gates, patch walls, pick up items |
| V | Show recipe — opens the formula list near workbenches and module slots |
| Hook Gun | Retrieves floating containers, pulls blue creatures in for Bug Slime, drags mechanical pivots |
| Axe | Breaks coral growths, crates and rotten doors |

## Fishing Controls: Mouse vs Keyboard

During the introductory fishing sequence you choose how the rod answers the fish:

- **Mouse** — intuitive for the opening Snappers; large desk movements needed when strong fish change direction.
- **Keyboard** — faster direction reversal once fish fight harder.

Both follow the same rule: the rod must oppose the fish's movement. If you keep losing pulls, switch schemes in the settings before blaming your gear — see the [fishing guide](/guides/fishing-guide/) for the stamina and tension side of the fight.
