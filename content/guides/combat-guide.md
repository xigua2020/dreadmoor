DREADMOOR is not a monster-hunting game, but it is not a pure fishing sim either. Creatures gate resources, punish greed, and make night travel a decision. This guide covers every threat in the demo and how to handle it — see the [creatures database](/world/creatures/) for the full bestiary.

## Ghouls: The Melee Lesson

**Ghouls** are the demo's basic land enemy, met in ruins and abandoned buildings. One has an axe in its head when you find it — they were people once, and they hit like it.

- **Swing the axe, then back off** before the counter. Hit, step out, repeat.
- **Never trade hits.** Health does not regenerate quickly, so every exchange you lose stays lost.
- If two ghouls group up, retreat to a narrow spot where only one can reach you — or leave.
- The **Serrated Axe Edge** upgrade (2× Jaws, 1× Metal Plate, 2× Bug Slime, 1× Rope) makes forced encounters noticeably faster.

## The Giant Tentacle: A Hazard, Not a Boss

At night, a **giant tentacle** rises beside the boat and strikes it, dealing hull damage. There is no named boss fight in the demo — the tentacle is the sharp end of the night-travel risk:

- Keep the boat **moving**; do not linger on open water after dark.
- Stay inside and protect the **hull** — patch damage at the Dry Dock with Bug Slime.
- Plan routes so night water is a choice, not a surprise.

## Spitting Coral: Dodged, Not Fought

The spitting variety of coral launches projectiles when you come close. It is a hazard with a creature's behavior:

- **Time your crossings** between volleys.
- Never stand still in its range.
- Remember the glowing coral on Angler's Island is a different, harmless resource — learn to read coral patches before walking in.

## Blight Fog

**Blight Fog** damages you over time while you stand in it. There is nothing to fight — leave the area, use medicine if you were caught, and route around dense banks along the Blight Line.

## Your Fighting Tools

| Tool | Role | Upgrade |
|---|---|---|
| Axe | Melee and harvesting (coral, crates, doors) | Serrated Axe Edge |
| Hook Gun | Salvage, pulling blue pivots and creatures | Reinforced Hook Grip |

The Hook Gun is a tool first — it retrieves floating containers, pulls the small blue creatures in for Bug Slime, and works the mechanical pivots at The Throat. Keep both tools in good condition; combat encounters in the demo are short, and preparation decides them more than reflexes.
