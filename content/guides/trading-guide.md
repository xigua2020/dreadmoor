Your catch is your income — but in DREADMOOR, the smart money is not always the sale. This guide covers fish values, the sell-vs-process decision, and the two shop purchases that gate your boat.

## Fish Values

Fish sell **per kilogram** at Betsy and Sons, so heavier specimens pay more. Value tiers from the demo data:

| Tier | Value | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Common early fish | 1/kg | Bellybulge, Cryptmaw, Rammfish |
| Snapper | 4/kg | The first contract fish |
| Mid predators | 7/kg | Bully, Gleamer, Harpooner, Harpy |
| Softgrazer | 8/kg | Better processed than sold |
| Deep-water predators | 12/kg | Bloodgill, Greasechar |
| Sharpfin | 16/kg | The top of the ledger |

The full per-species table is in the [fish list](/fishing/fish-list/).

## Sell or Process?

The Grinder and Thresher turn fish into materials money cannot shortcut:

- **Softgrazer → Chromatic Scales** — hull protection, Rainbow Fin, Sturdy Earcrawler and the Spotlight all want scales. Process first, sell overflow.
- **Sharpfin → Sharpfin Flesh** — the Deadly Tick bait chain. A 16/kg sale is tempting; the flesh is rarer.
- **Predators → Jaws** — Flaming Belly and two tool upgrades compete for them.
- **Everything else** — grinding any fish makes Fish Meat, which feeds bait recipes and the Fuel Larva.

And the rule that overrides every calculation: **contract fish are never sold**. Deliver them whole.

## What to Buy

- **Carburetor (50)** — unlocks after the three-Harpooner delivery; required for the Upgraded Engine. The delivery only opens the listing — you still have to buy it.
- **Torn Pages (200 each)** — research currency, sold by traders when you are short of exploration finds. Buy only after funding the main-quest blueprints.
