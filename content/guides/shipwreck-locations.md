Salvage is DREADMOOR's second economy: fish pay the shop, but wrecks pay the workshop. This guide covers where the salvage lives, what shares the water with it, and how to run a route that comes home heavier than it left.

## The Salvage Fields

The Salvage Fields, out in the Salvage Shoals, are the densest source of **Metal Plates** in the demo — plus Gears, Machine Oil and Rubber. The method is simple:

1. Approach slowly; containers drift in clusters.
2. **Hook Gun** every floating barrel and metal container.
3. Watch the fuel gauge — the fields are far enough that the return leg matters.

Metal Plate is the game's biggest bottleneck (boat modules, repairs, bait and tools all want it), so a weekly salvage habit pays for itself. See every material's source in the [resources database](/world/resources/).

## Wrecks With Teeth

Three wreck-type habitats hold both salvage and predators:

- **Wreck fields** — **Rammfish** (mutant, Pipecross / Deadly Tick).
- **Broken hulls** — **Shardback** (mutant, Rainbow Fin / Flaming Belly).
- **Sunken structures** — **Swallower** (mutant, Rainbow Fin / Flaming Belly).

If you are already making the trip, bring predator bait: the fish are worth 1–7/kg and process into **Jaws** for tool and bait recipes.

## Tools of the Trade

| Tool | Salvage role |
|---|---|
| Hook Gun | Retrieves floating containers, pulls blue creatures (Bug Slime), drags mechanical pivots |
| Axe | Breaks crates, coral and rotten doors on foot |
| Lockpicks | Crude → standard → Master, for locked containers |

The **Reinforced Hook Grip** (1× Gear, 2× Jaws, 1× Bug Slime, 1× Rope) and **Serrated Axe Edge** (2× Jaws, 1× Metal Plate, 2× Bug Slime, 1× Rope) are the two tool upgrades that pay for themselves fastest on salvage runs.

## Route Discipline

A profitable salvage route is a fuel equation with a fishing bonus: tank full, hull patched, hook and axe ready, one bait for the local predators, and a hard rule about the return leg. The Submerged Lands charges for greed in the one currency you cannot salvage — distance.
