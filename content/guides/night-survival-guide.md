Night changes the water in DREADMOOR. New species rise, the light dies, and something large starts paying attention to your hull. Night runs are a decision — this guide makes it an informed one.

## What Changes at Night

- **Night-tied fish** — Harpy (mutant predator) and Gleamer are recorded in night water. If your ledger is missing them, daylight will not fix it.
- **The giant tentacle** — rises beside the boat and strikes it for hull damage. There is no boss fight; it punishes lingering, not traveling.
- **Visibility** — dark water hides splash zones, salvage and the route home.

![The giant tentacle is the night tax on open water](/images/night-fish-monster.webp)

## Preparing for a Night Run

1. **Full tank** — via the Fuel Gun, not just a fed larva.
2. **Patched hull** — Dry Dock, Bug Slime, every section.
3. **Repair reserves** — Rusty Scrap Metal and spare Bug Slime aboard.
4. **Right bait** — Rainbow Fin or Flaming Belly for the night predators.
5. **Spotlight** — if you have the module (4× Cable, 5× Coral Fibers, 3× Bug Slime, 1× Relay Tubes, 4× Chromatic Scales), night stops being a gamble.

## Night Fishing Tactics

The mechanics do not change after dark — splash zones still mark predators, stamina and tension still decide the fight. What changes is margin for error: you see less, and help is farther away. Fish the zones closest to your route home first, and keep the engine warm between catches.

## When to Turn Back

Turn around when the hull takes its first tentacle hit without repairs aboard, when the fuel gauge crosses the halfway mark before your farthest target, or when the hold is full — a full hold at midnight is a finished trip, not a failed one.
