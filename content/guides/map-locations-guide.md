The channel looks wider on the chart than it does at the helm. This guide maps the demo region of the Submerged Lands and the travel logic between its ten marked locations. Live data is in the [locations database](/world/locations/) and the [world map](/world/map/).

## The Regions

- **The Sunken Approach** — where you start: the Abandoned House and the run west to the Trading Post.
- **Trading Post** — the hub: Betsy and Sons, the Bunkhouse and the Dry Dock.
- **Salvage Shoals** — wreck-strewn water including the Salvage Fields.
- **Blight Water** — the Sea Blight's edge, marked by the Blight Line.
- **The Throat Basin** — the gate out of the region.

## The Ten Marks

| Location | Type | Why you go |
|---|---|---|
| The Abandoned House | Start | Opening supplies, the Axe, the radio |
| Betsy and Sons | Port | Contracts, shop, processing machines |
| The Bunkhouse | Port | Lodging, the Simple Rod |
| The Dry Dock | Workshop | Boat repairs and modules (John the Fifth) |
| Angler's Island | Salvage | Coral Fibers with the Axe |
| Peaceful Fishing Grounds | Fishing | Softgrazer contract, Wind-Up Tin water |
| Predator Splash Zone | Fishing | Harpooner contract, Rainbow Fin water |
| The Salvage Fields | Salvage | Metal Plates and mechanical salvage |
| The Throat | Gate | The way out — machinery puzzle |
| The Blight Line | Danger | Mutant fish, Blight Fog |

## Route Planning

Every trip is a fuel equation: check the tank at the dock, budget the return leg, and patch the hull before outer water. Angler's Island is the safe early run; the Salvage Fields pay best per trip; The Throat is the one-way objective at the end of the route — arrive with [Hull Protection and the Upgraded Engine](/guides/boat-upgrades-guide/) installed, then read the [Throat gate guide](/guides/the-throat-guide/) before docking.
