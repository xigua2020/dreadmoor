A fast engine does nothing for a boat taking water. This guide covers the two repair systems — hull and engine — and the material habits that keep you off the tow rope.

## Hull Repair at the Dry Dock

The hull is patched at the **Dry Dock**, and the workshop must be awake first:

1. Repair the **Fuse Box** with 1 Cable, then flip the separate **Power Switch** — two different interactions.
2. Raise the boat on the **Boat Lift**.
3. Run the **Pressure Pump** and blast the **Polyps** off with the **Water Cannon**.
4. Load **Bug Slime** into the **Repair Gun**.
5. Repair **every damaged hull section individually** — one fixed slot does not mean the hull is done.

![Patching damaged sections — hull work is section by section](/images/bunkhouse-repair.webp)

## Stocking Bug Slime

Bug Slime comes from the **small blue creatures** drifting on the water. Pull them in with the **Hook Gun** and gather a little extra on every trip — the Moonshine Still, hull patching and several recipes all draw from the same stock. See the [resources database](/world/resources/) for every material source.

## Engine Repair

Engines are simpler: **Rusty Scrap Metal** fixes them, no Dry Dock required. Budget 3 units per repair for Standard, Upgraded and Heavy-Duty engines, 4–5 for higher tiers. Salvage a reserve before long runs.

## Damage Prevention

- The **giant tentacle** strikes at night — plan routes so dark water is a choice.
- Slow down near docks and wreck fields; collision damage is self-inflicted.
- **Hull Protection** (Protection 2, Speed −1) reduces risk on the main route but never replaces repairs.
