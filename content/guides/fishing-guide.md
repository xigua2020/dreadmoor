DREADMOOR's fishing is a first-person fight. You read the water, choose the lure, keep the rod still for a peaceful bite, then pull against a fish that wants the line back. This guide covers the whole loop — browse the [fish list](/fishing/fish-list/) for per-species data and the [bait database](/fishing/bait/) for recipes.

## The Six-Step Fishing Loop

1. **Interact with the spot.** The prompt opens the tackle screen and shows which fish are active there.
2. **Choose rod and bait.** The Simple Rod finishes the opening contracts; bait is the part that matters.
3. **Cast into the zone.** Aim at the active water — for predators, inside the splash zone.
4. **Wait or retrieve.** Peaceful fish need a still rod; predators need a moving lure.
5. **Fight stamina and tension.** Pull opposite the fish, reel when it tires, ease off when the line strains.
6. **Land and keep.** Use the Land Fish prompt when the fish is close — and keep contract fish whole.

## Stamina: Tiring a Fish Without Losing It

A hooked fish swims against the rod. Pull in the opposite direction to drain its **Stamina**; if it changes direction, change with it. When Stamina hits zero the fish is exhausted but not caught — you still have to bring it in, and it can recover during the retrieval. For reference, a class 3 Snapper has 100 stamina, a class 3 Softgrazer 110, and a class 3 Sharpfin 120.

## Line Tension: Why Lines Snap

**Line Tension** is the strain meter. Reeling against an actively fighting fish raises it; so does letting a fish run far from the boat. At maximum, the line snaps. The dangerous moment is usually the final retrieval — the fish looks close and makes one more run. Ease off, let it take line, then resume. Losing a metre is cheaper than losing the catch. A **Braided Line** upgrade gives more margin but not immunity.

## Bait by Temperament

Bait is a category, not a flavour. Using the wrong category usually means no bite at all.

| Bait | Type | Best for | Recipe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earthworm | Universal | Snapper only | Starting bait |
| Wind-Up Tin | Peaceful | Softgrazer contract | 1× Metal Plate + 1× Fish Meat |
| Sturdy Earcrawler | Peaceful | Trophy peaceful fish | 1× Metal Plate + 1× Battery + 1× Thermo Coil + 1× Chromatic Scales |
| Rainbow Fin | Predatory | Harpooner contract | 1× Fish Meat + 1× Metal Plate + 1× Chromatic Scales |
| Flaming Belly | Predatory | Trophy predators | 1× Fish Meat + 1× Jaws + 1× Metal Plate + 1× Lily |
| Pipecross | Predatory | Bonescar, Bully, Rammfish, Stonegnawer | No workbench recipe |
| Deadly Tick | Predatory | Same narrow group | 1× Sharpfin Flesh + 1× Circuit Board + 2× Sturdy Metal + 1× Cable |

## Predator Fishing: The Splash Zone

Predators do not take a stationary bait. Look for disturbed water — the **splash zone** — cast inside it, and start retrieving immediately. Keep a steady pace: reel too fast and the predator cannot catch the lure; leave it still and it loses interest. If a fish follows and turns away, recast with a different pace rather than swapping bait.

## The Three Contract Catches

- **Five Snappers** — Simple Rod and Earthworm at the platform behind the Dry Dock. The tutorial fight.
- **Three Softgrazers** — Wind-Up Tin at the marked peaceful spot. Deep stamina pool; keep all three whole for the Thresher, which turns them into **Chromatic Scales**.
- **Three Harpooners** — Rainbow Fin in a splash zone. Delivery unlocks the **Carburetor** purchase at Betsy and Sons — you still have to open the shop and buy it for 50.

## Gear Upgrades: What to Craft First

| Upgrade | What it changes | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Braided Line | Stronger line, fewer break-offs | High |
| Reinforced Rod | Tires fish faster | Medium |
| Baitcasting Reel | Faster line recovery | Medium |
| Sturdy Earcrawler | Peaceful trophy chance | Optional |
| Flaming Belly | Predator trophy chance | Optional |

The Simple Rod can finish the demo — upgrades make fights forgiving, not possible. Fix input habits before spending materials.

## Processing: Grinder, Thresher and Fuel Larva

Three machines turn a catch into supplies: the **Grinder** makes Fish Meat for bait and fuel, the **Thresher** processes peaceful fish like Softgrazer into Chromatic Scales, and the **Fuel Larva** eats Fish Meat and produces fuel, which you transfer to the boat with the **Fuel Gun**. Producing fuel and filling the tank are separate actions — check the tank before every long trip.
