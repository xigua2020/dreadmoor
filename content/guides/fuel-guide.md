Fuel is the quiet timer under every DREADMOOR trip. The boat burns it, and the only production chain in the demo runs through a larva in a trader's workshop. Here is the whole loop and how to plan around it.

## The Fuel Production Chain

1. **Grind fish** — process caught fish at the Grinder into Fish Meat.
2. **Feed the Fuel Larva** — the larva eats Fish Meat and produces fuel over time.
3. **Fill the tank** — use the **Fuel Gun** to transfer the produced fuel into the boat.

Step 3 is the one everyone misses the first time: feeding the larva does **not** fill the boat. If the tank shows empty after feeding, you skipped the Fuel Gun.

![Approaching the dry dock settlement, where the fuel chain lives](/images/settlement-drydock.webp)

## Trip Planning

The Submerged Lands punish optimistic routing:

- **Check the gauge at the dock**, not at the horizon.
- Long legs — Angler's Island, the Salvage Fields, The Throat — deserve a full tank and a patched hull.
- Budget the **return leg** before chasing one more salvage container.
- Faster engines burn the distance down but do not change the rule: fuel out and back.

## The Fuel Tank Module

The cheapest insurance in the ship tree: the **Fuel Tank** costs 1× Metal Plate at the Ship Upgrades Table and stretches every fill. It competes with nothing important — craft it once the required modules are funded. See the [boat upgrades database](/boat/upgrades/) for the full module list.
