The Submerged Lands do not want you in them. Survival in DREADMOOR is preparation: the right treatment, a fueled and patched boat, and honest decisions about when a route is not worth the risk.

## The Sea Blight and Essence

The Sea Blight is the world's corruption — it twists fish into mutants and wears people down. At Betsy and Sons, **Gill and Bozur** administer an **Essence** injection that suppresses its effects. It is not charity: the treatment is recorded as a **debt** you repay through work, and that debt drives the opening act. Accept it, then work it off.

Out in the world, the Blight becomes physical. **Blight Fog** banks along the Blight Line damage you over time if you linger inside — leave the area and use medicine rather than pushing through.

## Fuel: The Two-Step Tank

The boat burns fuel, and the demo has exactly one production chain:

1. Process fish into **Fish Meat** at the Grinder.
2. Feed the **Fuel Larva** — it produces fuel.
3. Transfer the fuel into the boat with the **Fuel Gun**.

Feeding the larva does **not** fill the tank. If the boat shows empty after feeding, you skipped the Fuel Gun. Check the gauge before every departure; the run to Angler's Island or The Throat is a bad place to discover a dry tank.

## Repairs: Two Materials, Two Systems

- **Engine damage** — repaired with **Rusty Scrap Metal** (3–5 per repair depending on engine tier).
- **Hull damage** — patched at the Dry Dock with **Bug Slime** loaded into the Repair Gun.

Keep a small reserve of both aboard. A fast engine does nothing for a boat that is taking water.

## Night Water

Night changes the water. Species like **Harpy** and **Gleamer** are night-tied, and the **giant tentacle** rises to strike the hull after dark. Night runs are a decision, not a formality:

- Patch the hull and fill the tank first.
- A **Spotlight** module helps if you have one.
- Know your return route before you need it.

## The Pre-Departure Checklist

1. Fuel tank full (Fuel Gun, not just the larva).
2. Hull patched at the Dry Dock.
3. Rusty Scrap Metal and Bug Slime aboard.
4. Correct bait for the target water — peaceful or predatory.
5. Contract fish space in the hold; keep deliveries whole.
6. Journal objective read before casting off.
