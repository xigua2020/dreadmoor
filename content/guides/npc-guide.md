The demo has a small cast and a long memory. These are the faces behind the contracts, the Dry Dock key, and the shop listing that decides whether your engine gets fixed.

## Malcolm — The Player

Malcolm is a weathered fisherman with a past he does not explain and a boat that needs more work than he does. Voiced in first person, he is shown through his hands and tools rather than an avatar — you are not commanding the boat from above; you are standing on it, hauling lines and walking into ruins.

## Gill and Bozur — Betsy and Sons

The traders at the trading post are the first people to offer Malcolm help — and the first to make sure he remembers it. Their **Essence** treatment keeps the Blight at bay but creates the debt that structures the opening act.

**Services:** Essence treatment, the Bunkhouse key, the three fishing contracts (5 Snappers, 3 Softgrazers, 3 Harpooners), bait blueprints, general goods, fish sales, and the **Carburetor** purchase (50, after the Harpooner delivery).

## John the Fifth — The Dry Dock Engineer

John controls the **Dry Dock**, and when you meet him his Moonshine Still is broken and his patience is shorter than the repair list. He will not hand over the **Dry Dock Key** until the still works — which sends you to Angler's Island for Coral Fibers and onto the water for Bug Slime.

After the key, John becomes the boat progression guide: fuse box, hull cleaning, hull repair, the research tree, Hull Protection and the Upgraded Engine. If a crafted module seems missing, it is usually in the Dry Dock storage locker.

## Who Gives What

| Character | Location | Role | Quests & Services |
|---|---|---|---|
| Malcolm | The boat and on foot | Player | Fishing, salvage, repairs, combat |
| Gill | Betsy and Sons | Trader | Essence, contracts, shop, Carburetor |
| Bozur | Betsy and Sons | Trader | Essence, contracts, shop, Carburetor |
| John the Fifth | Dry Dock | Engineer | Moonshine Still, Dry Dock Key, boat modules |

**Ask about** contracts, blueprints, the Carburetor and the next boat module. **Bring** whole contract fish, Metal Plates, Bug Slime and Chromatic Scales. **Never** process a delivery before the hand-in.
