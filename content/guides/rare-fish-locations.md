Twenty-six of the ledger's twenty-eight species will come to you with routine work. These are the ones that will not — where they hide, what they take, and which are worth the trip. Full data for every entry is in the [fish list](/fishing/fish-list/).

## Sea Blight Mutants

Ten species carry the Blight. Their shared rule: **blighted water only, few habitats each**.

| Mutant | Habitat | Level | Bait |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glowbug | Blighted backwater | 0 | Rainbow Fin / Flaming Belly |
| Gravedigger | Drowned ruins | 0 | Wind-Up Tin / Sturdy Earcrawler |
| Necrosoft | Deep silt beds | 0 | Wind-Up Tin / Sturdy Earcrawler |
| Rammfish | Wreck fields | 0 | Pipecross / Deadly Tick |
| Sawfin | Coral margins | 0 | Wind-Up Tin / Sturdy Earcrawler |
| Shardback | Broken hulls | 0 | Rainbow Fin / Flaming Belly |
| Crackback | Blighted channels | 10 | Rainbow Fin / Flaming Belly |
| Harpy | Night water | 10 | Rainbow Fin / Flaming Belly |
| Stonegnawer | Concrete ruins | 10 | Pipecross / Deadly Tick |
| Swallower | Sunken structures | 10 | Rainbow Fin / Flaming Belly |

## Night-Only Fish

**Harpy** and **Gleamer** are the night-tied records. There is no daylight substitute: plan a deliberate night run with a patched hull, a full tank and — if you have it — a Spotlight module. The [night survival guide](/guides/night-survival-guide/) covers the preparation; the giant tentacle covers the price of skipping it.

## The Narrow-Bait Four

**Bonescar, Bully, Rammfish and Stonegnawer** ignore the standard predator lures. They are listed only for **Pipecross / Deadly Tick** — and Deadly Tick itself is a late project (5 Torn Pages research, plus Sharpfin Flesh in the recipe). Treat these four as a chain you unlock, not fish you stumble into.

## The High-Level Trio

- **Bloodgill (Lv 30)** — deep channels, 12/kg.
- **Greasechar (Lv 30)** — grease-stained water, 12/kg.
- **Sharpfin (Lv 50)** — deep peaceful water, 16/kg, 120 stamina at class 3. The hardest fight in the ledger and the source of **Sharpfin Flesh** — process it rather than selling when you can.

Level is a gate, not a difficulty slider: if a species is not appearing, your boat and gear have not reached its water yet.
